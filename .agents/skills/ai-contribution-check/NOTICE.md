They were modified for Physlib, first in
<https://github.com/leanprover-community/physlib/pull/1934>: the verdicts (`approve`,
`request_changes`, `block`) are replaced by an advisory score from 0 to 5, findings are phrased
as constructive suggestions, the Tau Ceti roadmap is replaced by the Physlib contribution rules,
and a physical relevance rubric is added. The rubrics were then rewritten as numbered tips
addressed to contributors, and the shared protocol was adapted to check a change against a tip
file. `render.py` and `SKILL.md` are adapted from the `ai-notes` skill of that pull request.
